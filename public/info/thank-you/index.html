Thank you to our supporters

The businesses and people who have helped the guild with donations of material, money and labour.

Over the years these businesses and individuals have donated their time, labour, materials and money to the guild. Please thank them by giving them your business.

If you’ve helped the guild out and we’ve missed you here, please let us know so we can add you. And if you’d like to support the guild yourself, we’d love to hear from you.
